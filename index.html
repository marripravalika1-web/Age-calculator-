LEARN THE BASICS

Calculation guides

How to calculate a percentage

To find what percentage A is of B, divide A by B and multiply the result by 100. For example, 25 out of 100 is 25%.

How age is calculated

Age is the time between a person's date of birth and a chosen date. A complete age calculation accounts for whether the birthday has occurred in the current year.

Understanding loan EMI

EMI is a fixed periodic payment that includes principal and interest. The estimate depends on the loan amount, annual interest rate and repayment duration.

What are CGPA and SGPA?

SGPA generally represents performance in one semester, while CGPA represents cumulative performance across semesters. Follow your institution's official calculation rules.
